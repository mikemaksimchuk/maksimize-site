🧭 Directions & Overview
"Assessing with Respect" 
By Starr Sackstein
Thank you for joining this professional learning experience.
This study plan follows the six chapters of Assessing with Respect by Starr
Sackstein. Each chapter explores how assessment practices influence students’
social and emotional development and how educators can create learning
environments where assessment promotes dignity, growth, and student ownership.
Participants are encouraged to engage with the book as part of this experience. You
may borrow a copy, access it in a way that works for you, or participate fully
through the reflection prompts and application activities provided here. Engagement
with the ideas—and thoughtful reflection on your own classroom context—is what
matters most.
This professional learning experience centers on the idea that assessment is not just
an academic process—it is also a relational one. The way educators design and
communicate assessments influences students’ confidence, motivation, emotional
responses to feedback, and willingness to take intellectual risks. By approaching assessment with empathy and
intentionality, teachers can build classrooms where students feel respected, supported, and empowered to take
ownership of their learning.
As you move through each chapter, record insights, identify questions or tensions, and reflect on how these
ideas connect to your own instructional practices. Dialogue with colleagues is encouraged as a way to extend
thinking and explore ways to build more humane and student-centered assessment practices.
Please don't hesitate to reach out if I can support you in any way to help you get the most out of this book study
experience. I'm excited to collaborate and make this journey both meaningful and enriching for you!

1️⃣Building Relationships to Support Learning
Chapter 1: Building Relationships to Support Learning
Big Idea
Strong teacher-student relationships create the foundation for meaningful assessment practices. When students
feel known and respected, they are more willing to engage with feedback and take ownership of their learning.
Reflection Questions
● Reflect on how relationships currently shape assessment practices in your classroom. In what ways do
students feel comfortable asking questions, sharing ideas, or responding to feedback?
● Think about a time when a strong relationship helped a student respond positively to feedback or
assessment results. What aspects of that relationship contributed to the outcome?
● How might students who feel disconnected from school experience assessment differently from students
who feel supported?
● What evidence do you have that your assessment practices communicate care, respect, and belief in
students’ abilities?
Application Activities
● Relationship Mapping: Reflect on your relationships with students and identify strategies for
strengthening connections with those who may feel less engaged.
● Student Conference: Hold a brief learning conference with a student to discuss their goals, progress,
and challenges.
● Feedback Reflection: Review recent feedback you provided to students and consider whether it
communicates encouragement, clarity, and respect.
2️⃣Developing Self-Awareness in the Context of
Chapter 2: Developing Self-Awareness in the Context of Assessment
Big Idea
Students develop stronger learning habits when they understand their strengths, areas for growth, and learning
processes.
Reflection Questions
1. Reflect on how students currently monitor their own learning in your classroom. What opportunities do
they have to reflect on their progress?
2. Consider how students respond to assessment results. Do they view assessments as judgments of ability
or as opportunities for growth?
3. What strategies might help students develop a deeper understanding of their own learning processes?
4. How might reflective assessment practices help students become more independent learners?
Application Activities
● Self-Assessment Activity: Ask students to evaluate their own work using a rubric or success criteria.
● Learning Reflection: Implement a short reflection prompt after an assignment asking students what
they learned and what they would improve.
● Goal Setting: Guide students in setting learning goals based on feedback from a recent assessment.
3️⃣Facilitating Self-Management for Better Learning
Chapter 3: Facilitating Self-Management for Better Learning
Big Idea
Students benefit from assessment practices that help them develop habits of organization, persistence, and
responsibility for their learning.
Reflection Questions
● Reflect on how students currently manage their learning in your classroom. What structures support
them in staying organized and engaged?
● Consider how assessment deadlines, feedback, and revision opportunities influence student work habits.
● How might assessment practices encourage students to take greater responsibility for planning and
completing their work?
● What strategies could help students develop stronger self-management skills?
Application Activities
● Progress Tracker: Introduce a tool that helps students monitor their learning progress.
● Revision Opportunity: Allow students to revise work after receiving feedback.
● Learning Plan: Ask students to create a short plan outlining how they will improve their work.
4️⃣Teaching Responsible Decision-Making
Chapter 4: Teaching Responsible Decision-Making to Promote Student
Ownership
Big Idea
Assessment practices can help students develop decision-making skills that promote ownership of their
learning.
Reflection Questions
● Reflect on the degree of choice students currently have in demonstrating their learning. How might
increased autonomy affect motivation?
● Consider how assessment structures influence student decision-making. Do students feel empowered to
make meaningful learning choices?
● What opportunities exist for students to evaluate their own progress and determine next steps?
● How might giving students greater responsibility in assessment processes strengthen engagement and
accountability?
Application Activities
● Choice-Based Assessment: Offer students multiple ways to demonstrate their understanding.
● Student-Led Conferences: Invite students to present their learning progress.
● Decision Reflection: Ask students to explain why they chose a particular approach to completing an
assignment.
5️⃣Understanding and Improving Emotional Responses
Chapter 5: Understanding and Improving Emotional Responses to Grades
Big Idea
Grades can trigger strong emotional responses that influence motivation, confidence, and engagement.
Thoughtful assessment practices can help students respond constructively to feedback.
Reflection Questions
1. Reflect on the ways students in your classroom respond to grades or evaluation. What emotional patterns
do you observe?
2. Think about how grading practices might influence students’ confidence and willingness to take
academic risks.
3. What strategies might help students focus more on learning and less on the emotional impact of grades?
4. How can teachers communicate feedback in ways that support resilience and growth?
Application Activities
● Feedback Conversation: Have a short conversation with students about how to interpret feedback
constructively.
● Grade Reflection: Ask students to reflect on what they learned from an assessment rather than focusing
solely on the grade.
● Growth Mindset Prompt: Encourage students to identify next steps for improvement after receiving
feedback.
6️⃣Reinforcing Student Dignity Through Personalized
Chapter 6: Reinforcing Student Dignity Through Personalized Assessment
Big Idea
Personalized assessment practices honor students’ individual strengths, needs, and learning paths while
maintaining high expectations.
Reflection Questions
● Reflect on how assessment practices in your classroom recognize students as individuals with unique
experiences and abilities.
● What opportunities exist for students to demonstrate learning in ways that reflect their strengths?
● How might personalized assessment practices promote equity and student dignity?
● What adjustments could help ensure that all students feel respected and valued during assessment
processes?
Application Activities
● Flexible Demonstration: Provide alternative options for demonstrating learning.
● Student Voice: Invite students to provide feedback about assessment practices in the classroom.
● Personal Learning Reflection: Ask students to identify the assessment strategies that help them learn
best.
🏁 Final Reflection and Action Plan
Final Reflection and Action Plan
Assessment practices influence far more than academic outcomes. They shape students’ identities as learners, their
confidence, and their willingness to engage with challenging ideas. When assessment practices emphasize relationships,
reflection, and respect, classrooms become spaces where students feel valued and motivated to grow.
By integrating social and emotional awareness into assessment practices, educators can create environments where
feedback supports learning, mistakes become opportunities for growth, and students feel empowered to take ownership of
their education.
