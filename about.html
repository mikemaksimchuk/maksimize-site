Meet Mike

Classroom roots.
A wider view.

My work connects classroom practice, assessment, organizational learning, and artificial intelligence.

Educator, author, and AI strategist

I’m Dr. Mike Maksimchuk, an AI Strategist with Michigan Virtual. Before that, I supported assessment work at Kent ISD and taught science and engineering in Grant and White Cloud, Michigan.

Across those roles, one question has stayed with me: how can we make evidence of learning more useful to the people who need it?

Today, my work includes professional learning, assessment design, writing, and collaboration with educators in Michigan, across the United States, and internationally.

Education & professional community

  • M.Ed. — Concordia University Portland, 2015
  • B.S. — Michigan Technological University, 2009
  • Michigan educator and school administrator credentials
  • NCME classroom assessment committee service and AI4MI collaborative leadership
  • VISION collaboration with the European Center for Multilingualism in Teacher Education
