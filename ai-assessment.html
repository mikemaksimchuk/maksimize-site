Purpose before possibility

Human judgment.
Stronger evidence.

AI can support teaching and assessment when we remain clear about what students are learning and what the evidence can tell us.

Instructional purpose

Start with learning

Name the knowledge or skill you want students to demonstrate before choosing a tool or generating a task.

Validity & clarity

Protect the meaning of evidence

Ask whether AI assistance changes what the task measures. Make expectations for student and educator use explicit.

Equity

Design for access and agency

Consider whose language, experiences, and ways of showing learning are supported—and who might encounter barriers.

Responsible practice

Keep people responsible

Review outputs, protect student information, and use professional judgment when interpreting evidence or providing feedback.
